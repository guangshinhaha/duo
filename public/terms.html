What this is

Digital Duo is an experimental pilot, run for a small group by invitation. It's provided as is, with no guarantee of availability, accuracy or data retention. It may change or shut down at any time.

Your duo is not you

Your duo drafts; you decide. Drafts can be wrong. You're responsible for anything you send. Anything your duo answers through an ask link is labelled as coming from your duo, not from you.

What you may put in

  • Only conversations you have the right to use.
  • No classified or sensitive government material, and nothing above Official (Open). This pilot runs on third-party infrastructure and a third-party AI service.
  • No one else's personal data beyond what's incidental to your own conversations.

Access

Invites are personal and single-use. The administrator may remove access or delete a duo, for example at the end of the pilot, and will give notice where practical.
