Privacy policy

Digital Duo, an invite-only pilot. Last updated 26 September 2026.

Digital Duo lets you build an AI "duo" from your own past conversations. The duo drafts replies in your voice, and you review them. This page says what we store, where, and who can see it.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your Google account's stable ID, email address and name. We use them only to sign you in and to label your duo. We don't request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else.
  • What you give your duo: the conversation history you import (a Claude export or pasted transcripts): your messages and the AI assistant's replies in them. From sources with other people in them, only questions put to you are kept; anything else they said is discarded at import. Also the rules and voice profile derived from it, the drafts it writes, and your review decisions.
  • What people ask your duo through ask links you create, and what it answered. This is your duo's audit log.
  • Technical data: the devices you've signed in on and when each was last seen.

Where it's stored

Each duo has its own encrypted store (AES-256-GCM) on a server hosted by Railway. The server holds the keys so your duo can work while you're away. That means the administrator of this pilot can technically read your duo's contents. We don't do this except to fix a problem you've reported, but you should know it's possible.

Who else sees it

  • OpenRouter and the AI model provider it routes to receive the text needed to write each draft: the question, plus relevant excerpts of your history. They do not receive your whole corpus.
  • Railway hosts the servers.
  • Google handles sign-in.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Your choices

  • Sign out, and revoke any signed-in device, from Settings.
  • End your duo from Settings. This deletes its store and derived files. An administrator can also delete it on request.
  • Don't import material you aren't allowed to share with third-party services. This pilot is not approved for classified or sensitive government data.
