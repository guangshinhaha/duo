The loop

One question at a time, it gets closer to how you'd answer.

01

Someone asks you

In a conversation, a channel, or through an ask link you shared.

02

Your duo drafts

A reply only you can see, with the sources and rules it used beside it.

03

You decide

Send it, edit it, or ignore it. One key each. Nothing leaves until you send.

04

Your edit becomes a rule

What you changed becomes a standing order you approve, in plain words you can read.

Useful in the first hour. It answers on its own only when you let it.

What it learns from

A knowledge base isn't documents. It's decisions.

A search engine with your name on it isn't worth asking. What makes a duo useful is knowing what you'd say no to, what you'd push back on, and what you'd escalate.

Conversations

Teach what you decide.

Decisions in context, refusals, and who asks you what. Only your own words are kept. What others said is read for context, then dropped.

AI chat history

Teaches how you think.

When you talk to an AI you spell out your reasoning, your standards, and why you rejected an option. It shapes voice and judgment and is never quoted as something you said.

Docs and circulars

Teach what is true.

SOPs, runbooks, and the authoritative answer, so the duo can point to them rather than paraphrase them.

Trust

Promote slowly. Demote fast.

Autonomy is never one global switch. You grant it per topic and per kind of action, and your duo gets more careful the moment it's wrong.

What it will never do

A second you, labelled as one.

Always labelled

Everything your duo writes sits behind its line and under its name. It never uses your avatar and never speaks as you.

why?

Why costs zero clicks

Every draft shows the sources and standing orders it used, right beside it. You can point at the line that caused it.

T4

Nothing irreversible on its own

Anything external or binding needs your explicit confirmation at the moment it happens. That's a product principle, not a setting.

you

Only your own words

Other people's messages are read for context and then discarded. Direct messages stay out unless you add them one at a time.

END

Yours to end

You create your own duo. Nobody makes one of you. You can end it at any time, and what it learned goes with it.

log

A full record

Every answer traces to a source and every action to a rule, in a log only you can read.

Built for the person everyone asks, who rotates out within a year.

Your day is forty interruptions, most of them repeats. Your duo drafts the repeats so you can spend your attention on the questions that are actually new.

When your posting ends, your successor doesn't start from zero. They can consult the duo of the person who held the role before them.
